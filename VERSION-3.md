# VERSION 3 DESIGN — THE MISSING RESEARCHER

**Branch:** `version3-design`

**Website language:** English

**Status:** Active design direction

## Concept

Version 3 presents the portfolio as a lightweight 3D room exploration. The
visitor looks around Alina's active laboratory and clicks the computer, drawer,
notebook, books, note board, and field case. The computer becomes a full-screen
desktop; the other five objects become interactive local 3D scenes before any
individual page, file, note, or draft is opened. A possible
absence is revealed gradually rather than announced.

## Investigation structure

1. Enter Lab 17 and look around the single 3D room.
2. Drag the view and click highlighted objects to move closer.
3. Inspect six objects in any order. In the five physical stations, first enter
   a modeled close-up and then select the individual evidence inside it.
4. Discover the lab bulletin inside the computer and follow later physical clues.
5. Activate the printer and receive Alina's off-site field report after all six
   traces are logged.

## Design identity

- Realistic late-night creative laboratory rendered as a lightweight 3D scene.
- Deep ink, warm paper, signal lime, evidence red, and muted cyan.
- Editorial case files combined with tactile physical evidence.
- Notes, corrections, crossed-out routes, and quiet humor reveal personality.
- Real projects and experiences are the primary evidence.
- The mystery is inviting, intelligent, and safe rather than threatening.

## Development rule

This is an independent design branch. Versions 1 and 2 remain separate and
must not be changed while developing Version 3.
